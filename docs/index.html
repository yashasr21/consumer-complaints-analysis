What makes a consumer complaint escalate

Most complaints close quietly. A minority turn into disputes, and those cost far more to resolve. This looks at which ones, using the complaint text that arrives on day one. Built by Yashas R.

The model

Logistic regression on hand-built text flags, categorical context and TF-IDF terms. Trained on the earlier period and tested on the later one, never split at random.

What this does not tell you. The dispute field was retired part-way through the database, so this covers only the years where both a narrative and a dispute outcome exist. Complaints reaching CFPB are not a random sample of unhappy customers, and a dispute is a recorded action, not a measure of who was actually wronged.
